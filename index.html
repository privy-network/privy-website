Core architecture, chain configuration, and development environment.

02 — Internal Testnet

Local validators, transactions, staking, and smart-contract testing.

03 — Public Testnet

Community testing, infrastructure checks, and security improvements.

04 — Mainnet Preparation

Genesis validation, validator readiness, audits, and operational planning.

05 — Mainnet

Launch when the network is technically and operationally ready.
